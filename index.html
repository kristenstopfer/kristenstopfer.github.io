about kristen

Kristen is a Pennslyvanian born & raised, the youngest of 3 siblings, an avid crafter, a lover of the outdoors, and a lover of learning. She holds a B.A. in Political Science and a B.S. in Psychology from the University of Pittsburgh, and she is currently a Master's candidate in Human Development and Family Sciences at Texas Tech University. She is currently working on her thesis titled "Early Life Indicators of CVD Risk: Perceived Stress, Blood Pressure, and Blood Pressure Variability Across the Life Course in the NHLBI Growth and Health Study."
